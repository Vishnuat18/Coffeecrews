Project Overview

02 // The Challenge

Problem Statement

A significant "skill gap" exists between traditional university curricula and the fast-evolving needs of the tech industry. Students often find themselves technically unprepared for modern recruitment cycles, while companies struggle to identify ready-to-deploy talent among fresh graduates.

03 // The Strategy

Solution Approach

Aligned Learning

Curating content that directly mirrors the tech stacks and workflows used by leading software firms.

Mock Simulation

High-fidelity interview simulations with Al-driven feedback loops to refine student performance.

Direct Bridge

Automated matching systems that connect top-performing students directly to corporate recruiters.

09 // Roadblocks

Challenges Faced

  • Real-time Evaluation: Building a secure, isolated environment to compile and test student code in real-time.
  • Concurrent Engagement: Optimizing video streaming services for large-scale mock interview bootcamps.
  • Recruiter UX: Designing a filtered, data-rich view for recruiters that highlights talent without information overload.
